Our Story

Family-run, town-loved.

The Plane Street Coffee House & Cafe is a family-operated gathering place right in the heart of Bethel. It's the kind of spot where the person behind the counter knows your order before you say it — and where a cup of coffee turns into an hour of catching up with neighbors.

From made-to-order espresso drinks to griddle classics and homemade desserts, everything here is built around the same idea: good food, good coffee, and a seat that feels like home.
